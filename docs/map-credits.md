# Map and logo assets

## North America map

- Local asset: `public/images/north-america.svg`, viewBox `0 0 840 720`.
- Geographic source: [Natural Earth, 1:110 million Admin 0 countries](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/110m-admin-0-countries/).
- Data downloaded from the [Natural Earth vector repository](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson), accessed September 10, 2026. Major lakes use the matching [1:110 million lakes data](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_lakes.geojson), cut transparently out of the map.
- Licence: public domain. [Natural Earth terms of use](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) permit modification and commercial electronic use without permission.
- Made with Natural Earth. Primary authors include Tom Patterson and Nathaniel Vaughn Kelso.
- Cartographic treatment: spherical Albers equal-area conic projection, central meridian 98° W, origin 38° N, standard parallels 20° N and 60° N. Actual source country geometry is retained, with coordinates rounded only after projection. The continental viewport omits Hawaii and the outer Aleutian islands. Greenland and nearby countries provide subdued geographic context.
- Blue Canada, lighter blue United States, and subdued Mexico provide broad regional emphasis. No invented route lines, terminals, or offices are shown. Colour does not confirm any particular lane or service availability.
